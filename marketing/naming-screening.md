# Naming decision and screening record

**Status:** Bounded brand-name research, not legal clearance.

**Last bounded screen:** 2026-08-02.

This record separates creative preference, public-use evidence, inaccessible
sources, and final decisions. `CLEAR_FOR_PROTOTYPE` means only that the bounded
screen found no material conflict. It does not mean registrable, protectable,
or legally cleared.

## Decision sequence

1. `AI Booster Kit` was retained as a development working title.
2. The category was fixed as **Human-controlled AI Work Platform**, with the
   promise **Clarity and control** and the personality **Confident Systems**.
3. Five coordinated Hungarian-international territories were created.
4. `ALAKZAT / SETWISE` and `IRANY / NORTHFRAME` became the first two strongest
   pairs; `VALTO / SHIFTLOOP` then won on wording, mood, and theme.
5. The `SAJAT / SOVRA` palette won on color and was combined with the
   controlled-evolution narrative.
6. The permanent paired-name architecture was rejected in favor of one
   accent-free international master name.
7. The retained single-name batch produced no `CLEAR_FOR_PROTOTYPE` result.
8. `EVOBISEN` was coined from founder and product source words, then rejected
   after a bounded exact and confusing-similarity screen.

## EVOBISEN source construction

`EVOBISEN` was intentionally coined from letters, syllables, sound, and
structure associated with evolution, intelligence, two founder given-name
fragments, a founder surname fragment, a spiritual or creator motif, and
success. The public repository preserves the construction method without
persisting the underlying personal names.

The intended surface meaning was controlled evolution with hidden founder DNA.
The planned pronunciation was Hungarian `e-vo-bi-szen` and English
`eh-voh-BEE-sen`.

### Confusing-similarity conflict

The exact form appeared clean, but **EVOBIS** is the unchanged first six
letters of `EVOBISEN`. EVOBIS is an active Danish technology business providing
Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365, Power Platform digitalization, automation,
project-process support, and Copilot Studio AI agents. This is both a close name
and a close commercial category. Primary evidence:

- [EVOBIS company and positioning](https://evobis.dk/);
- [EVOBIS platforms and Copilot Studio agents](https://evobis.dk/platforme/);
- [EVOBIS services](https://evobis.dk/ydelser/).

Unquoted `evobisen` search also tended to surface EVOBIS, creating immediate
search and recall ambiguity. Additional near forms exist in automation,
access-control, AI, and software markets, but EVOBIS alone is sufficient for the
decision.

### Language and pronunciation

The bounded language pass combined ordinary spelling inference with dictionary
checks; it was not a native-speaker panel or comprehensive cultural review.

- From Hungarian orthography, the single `s` does not encode the intended `sz`;
  the planned `e-vo-bi-szen` pronunciation therefore does not follow the
  spelling.
- English `BEE-sen` versus `BYE-sen` is a pronunciation-risk inference, not a
  measured audience result.
- Spanish `bisen` is a neutral form related to repeating an item or performance
  in the [RAE entry for `bisar`](https://www.rae.es/drae2001/srv/search?id=oqZAH3GvnVcRjj2UkspD).
- German `Bisen` is a rare plural of `Bise`, a north or north-east wind, in
  [Duden](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Bise).
- No obscene or culturally exclusionary meaning surfaced in the bounded
  searches. Broader cultural acceptance remains `UNKNOWN` until native-speaker
  review is performed in target markets.

### Verdict

**`EVOBISEN = STOP` for the master brand.**

The decision is driven primarily by the `EVOBIS + EN` structure and direct
technology-category overlap, and secondarily by pronunciation instability. The
clean exact-name observations do not outweigh confusing similarity.

## Constraints for the next candidate set

The next generation must:

1. produce one accent-free international master name;
2. remain pronounceable from the written form in Hungarian and English;
3. avoid `EVOBIS`, `EVOBI`, `BISEN`, and near `EVOBIS + suffix` structures;
4. avoid names whose main differentiator is the crowded standalone `EVO`
   prefix;
5. retain controlled evolution, intelligence, founder DNA, and success through
   less literal phonetic or structural encoding;
6. complete exact and confusing-similarity screening before visual attachment;
7. keep `RISK`, `UNKNOWN`, and inaccessible-source limitations visible;
8. obtain professional legal clearance before adoption.

No research in this record authorizes domain purchase, handle registration,
account creation, filing, contact, repository rename, or public brand launch.
