# Marketing prototype

This directory is the reviewable marketing and brand prototype for the AI
Booster Kit product. It preserves the explored directions, the selected
creative system, the naming screens, and the design elements without claiming
that a final public brand has been approved.

No wordmark, repository rename, domain purchase, handle registration, account
creation, trademark filing, or public launch is approved by this prototype.

## Safe prototype use

The following may be used for internal prototypes and review:

- the category, promise, personality, voice, and copy sequence;
- the name-agnostic open-loop and Human Checkpoint visual motif;
- the SOVRA-derived blue, lime, ink, white, slate, and line palette;
- the typography, spacing, radii, semantic states, accessibility rules, and
  component architecture;
- the Free-to-Advanced business hypothesis, funnel, channel roles, content
  pillars, and launch gates when labelled as hypotheses or direction.

Public-facing use requires a selected and legally cleared master name, final
vector assets, platform-specific export verification, and explicit publication
approval.

## Review contract

A future marketing change is reviewable only when it:

1. preserves the distinction between shipped capability and roadmap intent;
2. records naming evidence and unknowns without turning a bounded screen into
   legal clearance;
3. keeps human control, evidence, and safe stop states out of the paid feature
   wall;
4. uses semantic state labels and non-color cues in addition to palette color;
5. updates derived tokens when the canonical visual values change;
6. leaves production publication, account creation, domain purchase, and
   trademark action behind separate approvals.
