# AI Booster Kit Brand and Marketing Prototype

**Status:** Approved creative system, unresolved master name. Naming clearance,
production assets, repository rebranding, and public adoption have not started.

**Goal:** Turn the AI Booster Kit working title into a credible, bilingual, commercially viable brand system for individuals and small-to-medium product, engineering, and delivery teams while preserving the platform's human-owned, evidence-first operating model.

## Current decision state

- `AI Booster Kit` remains the project and repository working title.
- The product requires one accent-free international master name rather than a
  permanent Hungarian-English wordmark pair.
- `VALTO / SHIFTLOOP` remains the strongest creative narrative donor, not an
  approved public name.
- The command-and-agency palette derived from `SAJAT / SOVRA` remains the
  selected visual direction.
- `EVOBISEN` is rejected with a `STOP` verdict because the active `EVOBIS`
  technology brand creates a close name and category collision.
- The complete decision trail and bounded screening record is maintained in
  [naming-screening.md](naming-screening.md).

## Decision summary

The selected creative direction combines two explored territories:

- **Narrative, mood, and product theme:** the historical `VALTO / SHIFTLOOP`
  direction — controlled evolution and adaptability.
- **Color character:** the `SAJAT / SOVRA` command-and-agency palette — confident blue, authority lime, deep ink, and signal white.
- **Category:** Human-controlled AI Work Platform.
- **Lead promise:** Clarity and control.
- **Personality:** Confident Systems — decisive, technical, energetic, and visibly state-aware without becoming noisy or authoritarian.

`VALTO` and `SHIFTLOOP` were designed as coordinated market expressions of one
product. The later single-name decision supersedes that architecture, while the
controlled-evolution story, copy patterns, and open-loop visual motif remain
valid prototype inputs. Normal Hungarian prose retains correct accents.

Creative selection and bounded screening are not legal or commercial
clearance. A future master name requires domain, social-handle, company-name,
confusing-similarity, pronunciation, and relevant trademark screening before
any public rename or asset publication.

## Product and market foundation

The prototype is grounded in the [platform roadmap](../docs/project/roadmap.md)
and the [current delivery state](../docs/project/current-state.md), not in a
generic autonomous-Agent narrative.

The current product can truthfully present:

- a local, deterministic Controller recommendation flow;
- READY Quick Task, research, validation, refinement, and implementation
  formations within their stated limits;
- explainable recommendation output with explicit missing prerequisites;
- a late, three-choice Human Checkpoint;
- visible evidence, `UNKNOWN`, `READY_WITH_LIMIT`, and stop states;
- an ephemeral Quick Task Activation Package that does not claim host execution;
- local behavior with no connector call, external read, external write, or durable session persistence.

The platform direction adds tuning, explicit save-or-ephemeral choice, compact resumable state, evaluation and `evolve`, debugging support, and optional Jira–GitHub–Confluence synchronization only after their contracts, implementation, evidence, consent, and recovery boundaries are ready.

### Audience

| Audience | Primary job | Brand response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual practitioner | Select useful AI assistance without surrendering judgment or learning an orchestration stack. | A clear recommendation, visible reason, and valid no-Agent path. |
| Product team | Turn goals, scope, acceptance, and uncertainty into a reviewable work system. | Situation clarity, explicit decision points, and reusable formations. |
| Engineering team | Use Agent assistance with bounded inputs, outputs, tests, evidence, and failure states. | Technical precision without autonomy theatre. |
| Delivery team | Coordinate ownership, dependencies, fan-out/fan-in, and review without hiding responsibility. | Visible system state and human-owned consequences. |
| Small-to-medium cross-functional team | Reuse proven working setups while changing one capability at a time. | Controlled evolution, history, and recovery as the product matures. |

## Verbal identity

### Voice traits

| Trait | Required behavior | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decisive | State the recommendation, evidence, consequence, and next decision directly. | Commands that erase User agency. |
| Precise | Name the capability, state, boundary, and unknown. | Technical language used only to sound advanced. |
| Transparent | Separate current truth, roadmap intent, inference, and missing proof. | Fear, false certainty, or buried limitations. |
| Energetic | Use active verbs and forward motion. | Hype, constant urgency, or visual noise. |
| Human-centered | Address the User as the owner of the work and outcome. | Anthropomorphic Agents, AI employees, or replacement narratives. |

### Copy pattern

Product and marketing copy should usually follow this sequence:

1. **Situation:** what work or uncertainty is present.
2. **System:** which bounded formation or capability fits and why.
3. **Evidence:** what supports the recommendation and what remains unknown.
4. **Decision:** which human-owned next moves remain available.
5. **Evolution:** what may be saved, evaluated, or improved only when that capability exists.

### Preferred vocabulary

Use: `formation`, `work situation`, `recommendation`, `evidence`, `unknown`, `checkpoint`, `choice`, `controlled change`, `history`, `recovery`, `human-owned`, `optional`, and `with limit`.

Avoid: `autopilot`, `AI employee`, `replace your team`, `zero effort`, `fully autonomous`, `magic`, `never wrong`, `one-click transformation`, `guaranteed`, or any claim that a recommendation is execution.

## Claim architecture

### Current-claim language

- “Get an explainable formation recommendation for supported work situations.”
- “See the required inputs, evidence, unknowns, and limits before deciding.”
- “Accept the recommendation, choose another path, or continue without an Agent.”
- “Prepare a bounded, ephemeral Quick Task activation package without claiming execution.”
- “Use local, deterministic Controller behavior that performs no connector action.”

### Roadmap-only language

The following must be labelled as planned, upcoming, or directional until current-state evidence changes:

- host adaptation or Agent execution;
- generated or saved activation artifacts;
- durable session state and private history;
- custom formation tuning;
- evaluation and `evolve`;
- debugging recipes promoted to READY;
- shared team libraries, analytics, and permissions;
- Jira–GitHub–Confluence lifecycle synchronization;
- enterprise governance and hosted inference.

## Final visual identity

The name-agnostic lead identity keeps the open-loop evolution motif from the
SHIFTLOOP direction and applies the command-and-agency colors selected from
SOVRA.

### Logo concept

The mark is two offset, open paths forming a controlled loop around a visible checkpoint gap. The gap is essential: it represents the point where the system stops and human ownership becomes explicit. The mark must not close into an infinity symbol, imply endless autonomous execution, or resemble a generic refresh icon.

Required logo variants:

- horizontal mark plus the future approved master-name wordmark;
- stacked mark and wordmark;
- mark-only avatar and app icon;
- wordmark-only usage when the mark is already present;
- full-color, reversed, dark monochrome, and light monochrome versions.

Clear space equals one mark height. The full digital lockup must remain legible at 120 px width; the mark-only minimum is 24 px. Production logos must be vector SVG first, with PNG export only where a platform requires it.

### Color palette

| Token | Value | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `loop.ink` | `#090D18` | Primary dark canvas, authority, and high-contrast text. |
| `control.blue.500` | `#326BFF` | Core brand signal, large display accents, diagrams, and non-text emphasis. |
| `control.blue.650` | `#2B5FE8` | Accessible primary action surface with signal-white text. |
| `evolution.lime` | `#C8FF3D` | Human checkpoint, progress, selective highlight, and campaign accent. |
| `signal.white` | `#F7F9FF` | Primary light text and reversed wordmark. |
| `system.slate` | `#141925` | Cards and elevated operational surfaces. |
| `system.line` | `#293043` | Borders, dividers, and inactive controls. |

Verified prototype contrast pairs:

- `signal.white` on `control.blue.650`: 5.11:1;
- `loop.ink` on `evolution.lime`: 16.47:1;
- `signal.white` on `loop.ink`: 18.43:1.

The originally selected `control.blue.500` remains a brand primitive but is not
used behind normal-size white button text because that pair measures 4.26:1.
State meaning and labels are invariant across themes, but rendered foreground
and background pairs are theme-specific. Raw indicator colors may be used only
on a surface where their contrast has been verified; they never borrow meaning
from brand blue or lime.

### Semantic states

| State | Dark-prototype badge pair | Indicator on `loop.ink` | Additional cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `READY` | `#86EFAC` on `#052E16` | `#22C55E` | solid dot and explicit label |
| `RECOMMEND` | `#BFDBFE` on `#172554` | `#3B82F6` | solid dot and recommendation label |
| `READY_WITH_LIMIT` | `#FDE68A` on `#451A03` | `#F59E0B` | limit label and explanatory boundary |
| `UNKNOWN` | `#CBD5E1` on `#1E293B` | `#94A3B8` | dashed outline and missing-evidence text |
| `STOPPED` / `BLOCKED` | `#FECACA` on `#450A0A` | `#EF4444` | stop label and actionable reason |

All dark badge pairs exceed 9.8:1, and all indicator colors exceed 5.1:1 on
`loop.ink`. Color is never the only state indicator. Icons, labels, border
treatment, and explanatory text remain mandatory. A future light theme must
define and verify its own state pairs rather than placing these indicators on
`signal.white` by default.

### Typography

- **Display and headings:** Inter Tight or Inter Variable, 600–750 weight, tight tracking.
- **Body and interface:** Inter, 400–600 weight, 16 px minimum for long-form body copy.
- **System labels and evidence metadata:** IBM Plex Mono or Cascadia Code fallback, 400–600 weight.
- **Hungarian support:** every selected font and export must retain `áéíóöőúüű` glyph coverage.
- **Layout:** body copy remains within approximately 65–75 characters per line; all-caps is reserved for short system labels.

### Shape, imagery, and motion

- Use open paths, visible gaps, checkpoints, relation lines, operational grids, and modular formation diagrams.
- Prefer real work artifacts, system maps, bounded status panels, and human decision moments.
- Do not use robot portraits, glowing brains, humanoid assistants, magic particles, or decorative neural-network clichés.
- Use a 4 px spacing foundation, primarily 8–16 px corner radii, and stronger geometry for checkpoint surfaces.
- Use 150 ms color transitions, 200 ms positional transitions, and 300 ms maximum explanatory reveals.
- Do not use ambient looping animation. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and preserve all meaning without motion.

## Design-system architecture

The product uses a three-layer token model:

```text
primitive values
  -> semantic purpose
    -> component behavior
```

### Primitive layer

Contains raw color scales, 4 px spacing scale, type scale, radii, border widths, shadows, durations, and z-index. Brand replacement changes primitives only where visual character changes.

### Semantic layer

Contains meaning-based aliases such as background, surface, foreground, primary action, secondary action, focus, evidence, recommendation, unknown, limit, stop, and checkpoint. Light and dark mode override this layer rather than individual components.

### Component layer

Defines navigation, button, input, formation card, evidence panel, state badge, Human Checkpoint, dialog, table, pricing card, social card, and empty/error/unknown state behavior. Components may not reference raw hex values.

### Required product components

- app shell and responsive navigation;
- work-situation input and validation feedback;
- Controller recommendation summary;
- formation identity, scenario, weight, topology, prerequisites, and fit reasoning;
- evidence coverage and missing-proof panel;
- state badges for the repository evidence vocabulary;
- three-choice Human Checkpoint;
- explicit no-Agent continuation surface;
- stop and unknown explanation patterns;
- Free and Advanced pricing presentation;
- bilingual marketing CTA and social-template modules.

### Accessibility and responsive behavior

- WCAG AA: 4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text and UI components.
- Visible 2 px focus ring with 2 px offset.
- Keyboard access for all interactive controls.
- Text and icon cues in addition to color.
- `aria-busy`, `aria-invalid`, and explicit error/help relations where applicable.
- Four-column mobile and twelve-column desktop layout behavior.
- Hungarian and English text expansion must not truncate decisions, state labels, or consequences.

## Business model

### Founding Free

The proposed early-access model makes every capability that is actually shipped
available without payment-card capture or an artificial feature wall. Launch
timing and availability remain `NOT EXECUTED`. If adopted, the product will
communicate that a durable Free tier and paid Advanced tier follow without
promising that all future capabilities remain free forever.

### Durable Free

The Free tier retains the trust foundation:

- Controller assessment and explainable recommendation;
- starter public formation library;
- Human Checkpoint and no-Agent route;
- visible evidence, unknown, limit, and stop states;
- local or ephemeral use within supported boundaries.

Human control, recommendation reasoning, safety boundaries, and access to the User's current result are never monetized as premium safety.

### Advanced pricing hypothesis

The initial validation hypothesis is **EUR 19 per active User per month**, with no minimum seat count. It is a research hypothesis, not a published price commitment.

Advanced value becomes sellable only as each capability is implemented:

- compact save and resume;
- private history and recovery snapshots;
- custom formation creation and tuning;
- evaluation and controlled `evolve`;
- private or shared team formation library;
- ownership, dependency, export, and usage views;
- optional integrations after separate readiness and consent gates.

The first commercial hypothesis assumes bring-your-own host or model execution.
If the future product later pays inference costs, model usage receives an
explicit included allowance and transparent metered extension rather than an
unbounded-use promise.

The benchmark set for later price research includes
[GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot/plans),
[Linear](https://linear.app/pricing), [Cursor](https://cursor.com/pricing),
[Notion](https://www.notion.com/pricing), and
[Replit](https://replit.com/pricing). Their live prices must be refreshed before
any decision. Final pricing requires willingness-to-pay interviews and measured
activation, retention, and cost data.

### Commercial funnel

1. Discover a work pattern or formation through useful public content.
2. Reach first value through an explainable recommendation and completed Human Checkpoint.
3. Return for a second work situation or reuse a formation within 14 days.
4. Encounter a legitimate need to save, customize, share, or evolve.
5. Add active team members and shared private formations.

Primary metrics are visitor-to-recommendation activation, checkpoint completion, 14-day return, Free-to-Advanced conversion, paid active Users, and serving cost per active User.

## Social and go-to-market system

The global brand channels are English-first. Hungarian content is founder-led and locally adapted; it is not a line-by-line duplicate. Separate Hungarian and international account networks are deferred until one audience is large enough to justify independent editorial operation.

| Channel | Role | Recurring use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | Category creation and B2B trust | Founder perspective, decision frameworks, evidence stories, and later a focused newsletter. |
| GitHub | Technical proof and transparent community | Reproducible examples, releases, roadmap context, Discussions, Q&A, and formation feedback. |
| YouTube | Education and product demonstration | Formation teardown, 5–10 minute workflow demo, Shorts, and later live clinics. |
| Discord | Retention and co-creation | Open only after a repeat active cohort exists; use onboarding, scenario forums, clinics, and roadmap sessions. |
| Reddit | Listening and earned reach | Answer real product, engineering, and delivery questions; avoid campaign-style self-promotion and premature owned-community creation. |

Product Hunt is a launch event rather than an always-on social channel. Use it for a publicly usable Free beta and later for a materially new Advanced release, with product feedback and activation as the success measure.

### Five content pillars

1. **Work Situation → Formation:** explain why a particular setup fits or does not fit.
2. **Human Checkpoint:** show the decision before consequence.
3. **Evidence and UNKNOWN:** make missing proof useful rather than embarrassing.
4. **Controlled Evolution:** compare one measured change with its preserved prior state.
5. **Build with Limits:** publish current capability, known boundary, and next proof without roadmap theatre.

### Provisional recurring series

These editorial formats remain usable, but their titles must be reconsidered
after the master name is selected.

- **Before → After Loop:** one controlled system change and its evidence.
- **Checkpoint:** a consequential decision and the available human-owned routes.
- **Formation Field Notes:** why a formation fits a work situation.
- **Unknown by Design:** a missing input or unsafe assumption caught early.
- **Release with Limits:** what became READY and what explicitly did not.

## Risks and controls

| Risk | Control |
| --- | --- |
| The next master name is unavailable or confusingly similar. | Preserve the creative system independently from the wordmark; stop public adoption on material conflict. |
| Evolution language implies a shipped `evolve` feature. | Label current versus roadmap capabilities in every product and campaign surface. |
| Blue and lime become generic SaaS decoration. | Anchor them to the open-loop checkpoint geometry and use lime only for deliberate human-owned emphasis. |
| Brand color is mistaken for semantic status. | Keep status tokens invariant, labelled, and independent of the theme palette. |
| Localized wordmarks fragment recognition. | Use one accent-free master name; localize copy rather than product identity. |
| Discord or Reddit launches as an empty owned community. | Start with LinkedIn, GitHub, and YouTube; open retention communities only after repeat participation exists. |
| Pricing is published before value and serving cost are known. | Keep EUR 19 as a validation hypothesis until interviews and product metrics support it. |
